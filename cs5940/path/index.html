The CS5940 Quiz 2 path

Weeks 5, 6 and 7 first, then a week 4 refresher, then a full mock Quiz 2 in the same style as Quiz 1: scenario questions, "which component failed?", matching, select-all and one number to type. Each stage is short: read, check yourself, pass the stage test.

How it works

  • Steps. Each stage is a few short steps with a checkpoint question. The next step opens when you get the checkpoint right. Wrong twice and you see the explanation.
  • Stage tests. Each stage ends with a quiz-style test marked all at once. 80% unlocks the next stage (70% for the mock). Questions are drawn at random from a bigger bank, so retakes differ.
  • Short on time? Every stage has a Jump to the stage test button. Or press Revision mode at the bottom of this page to unlock everything and go straight to the mock quiz.
  • Each stage links to the full topic pages on the Model Map if you need more than the summary.

What Quiz 2 probably covers

Quiz 1 (20 October) covered weeks 1 to 3: 15 questions in about 30 minutes, mostly scenario-based multiple choice, plus matching, select-all and one typed number. Quiz 2 (17 November) should cover the topics taught since: week 4 (MDPs and RL), week 5 (deep RL and LLMs), week 6 (LLM-based agents) and probably week 7 (game theory). That’s a guess from the timing, so check Moodle for the official list. Expect the same style: a short scenario, then “what happens?”, “which is it?” or “which fix?”.

The route

What the route is built from

  • The week 4 to 7 lecture slides (Stathis, Mensfelt, Trencsenyi), including every poll question and group task with its answer.
  • Quiz 1, for the question style: one scenario per question, plausible distractors built from common confusions, at least one matching and one select-all question.
  • Wooldridge ch. 11 for week 7 (the only part of these weeks the book covers).
